Trips

Going away? Take the proof with you.

Lost luggage and stolen cameras are where claims get hard. Provance builds the packing list, checks it against your travel insurance, and gives you a trip pack you can open at the airport with no signal.

  • Packing list that remembers. Copy last trip's list, or pick valuables straight from your vault.
  • Checked against your policy. Enter your luggage and single-item limits and see what's over before you leave.
  • Gaps flagged. Anything packed without a receipt or photo gets called out while there's still time to snap one.
  • Dated trip pack. One PDF of everything packed, receipts and photos inside, saved on your phone.
  • If it goes wrong. A short checklist of what to record, so the claim starts with evidence, not guesswork.

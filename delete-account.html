Delete your Provance account

You can ask for your Provance account and its data to be deleted at any time. This page explains how, and what happens next.

How to ask

Email support@provance.online from the email address you sign in to Provance with, with the subject "Delete my account". If you write from a different address, we'll ask you to confirm from the account's address first, so nobody else can delete your records.

We'll reply to confirm, and finish the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your email address, display name and sign-in.
  • Everything stored in Provance Cloud under your account: records, photos, receipts and Evidence Packs.
  • The log of any AI room scans you ran (how many photos and items each had).

Records you shared with your household are deleted too, so export anything they still need first.

What isn't deleted by us

  • Records only on your phone. Provance works without an account, and anything kept only on your device is removed by deleting the app.
  • Purchase records. Google Play and RevenueCat keep records of subscription purchases as they're required to for tax and accounting. Provance never sees your payment details.
  • Your subscription. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Deleting some data, not your whole account

You can delete any record in the app yourself: it moves to Recently deleted, and you can remove it from there for good. To have other data removed without closing your account, email the same address and tell us what you'd like gone.
